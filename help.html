Trip Planner

How this app works

Trip Planner is a private site where a small group of friends plans a trip together: pick a destination, pin places to go, compare flights and stays, sketch out days, and — after the trip — share a recap. Nothing here books anything or fetches live prices; it's just where your group collects and compares what you've each found elsewhere.

Signing in

Sign in with the Google account the trip owner added you with. If you see "this account isn't on the trip list yet," it means your exact email hasn't been added — ask the owner to add it (lowercase, matching your Google account exactly).

If the sign-in popup never appears, your browser is probably blocking popups — allow them for this site and try again.

The tabs, in the order most groups use them

Overview
Set trip dates, add travelers, and add candidate destinations. Rank them Must / Nice / Skip, then whoever's excited clicks Choose this destination to lock it in.
Places
Pin food, sights, tours and more in the chosen city. The fastest way in: paste a Google Maps link and the name and pin fill themselves in.
Flights & stays
Each traveler logs their own flight options from their home city; the group logs stay options. Log a price whenever you check one — the app tracks whether it went up or down and shows a running total per person.
Days
Drag ranked places into loose day plans (well — click Add to day; there's no dragging). Each day can open as a walking route in Google Maps.
Recap
After the trip: mark what you loved, log actual spend next to the planned total, and optionally publish a shareable read-only recap page — no sign-in needed to view it.

Common snags

  • You just heard about an update but don't see it. Wait a minute or two, then hard-refresh the page (Mac: ⌘⇧R, Windows: Ctrl+Shift+R).
  • Everything says "You don't have access." Tell the owner — this means the site's permission rules need republishing on their end, not something you can fix.
  • The map is blank, or something looks broken. A reload fixes most of it. If not, open your browser's console (Chrome: Option+⌘+J on Mac, Ctrl+Shift+J on Windows) and send the red error text to the owner.
  • You pasted a Google Maps link and nothing filled in. Short share links (like maps.app.goo.gl) can't be read this way — open the link, copy the full address from your browser's address bar, and paste that instead.

What this app deliberately doesn't do

No live flight or hotel prices, no booking or payments, no photo uploads (photos live in a shared album you link to), no chat or notifications, and no currency conversion. Every price you see is one a person typed in after checking it themselves.
