Trip Planner

How this app works

Trip Planner is a private site where a small group of friends plans a trip together. Nothing here books anything or fetches live prices; it's where your group collects and compares what you've each found elsewhere.

Add to home screen and sign in

Make Trip Planner easy to find on your phone.

  1. On iPhone, tap Share → Add to Home Screen. On Android, tap the menu → Install app. This adds a shortcut that works like a real app.
  2. Open the app and sign in with your Google account, or create one with email and password.
  3. If you see "this account isn't on the trip list yet," ask the owner to add your email (lowercase, exactly as it appears in your Google account).
If it doesn't work
  • The sign-in popup never appears. Your browser is probably blocking popups — allow them for this site and try again.
  • Everything says "You don't have access." Tell the owner — the site's permission rules need updating on their end.

Routes: copy and paste flights

Start by pasting a Google Flights link. It fills in your home airport, dates, and which cities are in your trip.

  1. Go to Routes tab and tap Paste flight link.
  2. Open Google Flights and find a flight. The price must be visible on the link — paste the full address from your browser's bar, not a short share link.
  3. The app reads where you're from (your home airport), the date you leave, and where you arrive. Each traveler can paste their own flights from their home city on different dates.
  4. You'll see it as a home flight, which sets your trip window. Other travelers paste their flights too — they might leave on different days.
  5. Once everyone has a home flight into the region, paste flights between cities (hops). These create options: "Rome + Florence," "Madrid + Barcelona," etc.
  6. The app shows your route, how many nights, and the total cost per person.
If it doesn't work
  • Short share links don't work. Open the link in Google Flights, copy the full address from your browser's address bar, and paste that.
  • The price didn't read correctly. The link must have the price visible. Go back to Google Flights and make sure you copied the whole URL.

Check prices

Prices are a snapshot from when the link was copied. Check them later to see if they've changed.

  1. On a flight or stay, the price is a button. Tap it to see what Google showed when the link was pasted.
  2. If the price is the same, tap Same. If it changed, enter the new price and tap Save price.
  3. The app shows whether prices went up or down and keeps a running total per person.
  4. On the Routes or Map tabs, tap Check prices to walk through every price older than 3 days, one at a time.
If it doesn't work
  • No link was saved. The app still asks for a price, but can't open the site — check it yourself and enter what you see.

Map: places and stays by stop

Pin the restaurants, museums and hotels you want to check out. The map groups them by the cities in your route.

  1. Go to Map and tap Add place. Search or paste a Google Maps link — the name and location fill in automatically.
  2. You can also tap Import from My Maps to upload a Google My Maps layer (export it as KML from My Maps, not KMZ).
  3. To add where you're staying, tap Add stay and paste a Booking.com, Airbnb or Google Hotels link. The app fills in dates from your stops and you enter the price.
  4. The app shows night coverage for each stop — how many nights each stay covers and the total cost.
If it doesn't work
  • Short share links don't work. Open the link, copy the full address from your browser's address bar, and paste that.
  • KMZ files don't import. In My Maps, use ⋮ → Export to KML/KMZ and tick "Export as KML instead of KMZ", then try again.

Days: plan day by day

Organize your favorite places into a day-by-day plan.

  1. Go to Days and tap Create days from route. The app makes one day per city in your chosen route.
  2. Tap a place pin to add it to a day. The map shows which places are on which day.
  3. The app numbers each place, so you can sketch a walking route.

Take the plan to Google Maps

Download your day-by-day plan as a map file and open it in Google Maps for turn-by-turn navigation.

  1. On the Days tab, tap Take it to Google Maps to download a file.
  2. Open Google My Maps and tap Create a new map → Import. Pick your file; a layer per day appears with your places and stays.
  3. Open the map in the Google Maps app on your phone. Tap a place to get directions for that day's walking route.

Quick fixes

  • You just heard about an update but don't see it. Wait a minute or two, then hard-refresh the page (Mac: ⌘⇧R, Windows: Ctrl+Shift+R).
  • The map is blank, or something looks broken. A reload usually fixes it. If not, open your browser's console (Chrome: Option+⌘+J on Mac, Ctrl+Shift+J on Windows) and send the red error text to the owner.
